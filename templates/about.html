ABOUT IMPACT LAB • Architecture & Methodology

About IMPACT LAB

From Ideas to Tested Impact. An open problem-solving infrastructure built to bridge the gap between theoretical proposals and verified real-world relief.

Foundational Principle

Why IMPACT LAB Exists

Innovation competitions are often dominated by flashy presentations with no verification of whether the proposed solution actually fits real-world constraints or solves a genuine problem. Meanwhile, communities, NGOs, and municipal agencies face urgent operational bottlenecks that remain unsolved.

IMPACT LAB changes this paradigm: Every registered challenge requires explicit constraints, available resources, and verifiable definitions of success. Solutions are not ranked by subjective hype, but evaluated using mathematically grounded Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) and measured pilot testing.

Core Platform Modules & Three Primary Workflows

Engineered to handle diverse real-world situations with transparent backend logic.

Workflow A

Problem + Solution Idea

A user identifies a real friction point and has a concrete proposed idea. The system calculates an objective MCDA feasibility score across 6 weighted operational dimensions (impact, technical readiness, budget, deployment speed, resources, scalability) and deducts risk penalties.

Workflow B

Smart Solution Discovery (Problem but No Solution)

A user suffers from a severe bottleneck but lacks technical or design expertise. The system tokenizes their natural language input, filters stop words, extracts TF-IDF keyword weights, and matches them against proven solutions and engineering blueprints.

Workflow C

Need Implementation Help

A project lead has a viable blueprint but faces an execution hurdle (e.g., Python scripting, 3D CAD modeling, embedded sensors, or volunteer logistics). They post a targeted help request, connecting directly with verified student and expert solvers.

Technical Architecture & Formulas (For Reviewers)

Full algorithmic transparency without black-box obscurity.

📐

1. NLP Vector Space Modeling & Cosine Similarity

Solution Discovery Engine

Input text is normalized to lowercase, stripped of punctuation, and filtered through an English stop-word lexicon. Term frequency and smooth inverse document frequency are calculated across all documents:

IDF(t) = ln((1 + N) / (1 + DF(t))) + 1.0

Vectors are L2-normalized: ||v|| = √(∑ w_i²). Semantic relevance between problem query vector q and solution vector s is computed using cosine similarity:

cos(θ) = (q • s) / (||q|| × ||s||)

A hybrid score incorporates cosine similarity (45%), keyword set overlap (35%), shared keyword count bonus, and domain category alignment (15%).

⚖️

2. Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA)

Feasibility & Impact Engine

Proposals are rated on a normalized 1-10 scale across 6 orthogonal operational criteria with established weights summing to 1.0:

  • Expected Benefit & Impact: 25% (Max 25 pts)
  • Technical Feasibility: 20% (Max 20 pts)
  • Cost Fit / Budget Viability: 15% (Max 15 pts)
  • Implementation Timeline: 15% (Max 15 pts)
  • Resource & Skill Access: 15% (Max 15 pts)
  • Scalability & Replicability: 10% (Max 10 pts)
Base Score = ∑ (Rating_i / 10.0) × Max_Points_i

An explicit risk penalty scales linearly with uncertainty:

Risk Deduction = (Risk_Level - 1.0) × 1.5 pts
Overall Feasibility Index = max(0, min(100, Base Score - Risk Deduction))
Civic & Public Policy

Government & Civic Redress Integration

When a problem relates to public infrastructure (such as municipal waterlogging, broken roads, sanitation, or potable water), IMPACT LAB provides verified links to official statutory grievance portals:

CPGRAMS (DARPG, Central Grievances) Swachhata Urban (MoHUA, Municipal Redress) Jal Jeevan Citizen Portal (Ministry of Jal Shakti) National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (MHA) MyGov Innovation Platform (MeitY)

Transparency Guarantee: IMPACT LAB does not file legal complaints or claim government affiliation. It equips citizens and student teams with awareness of official escalation pathways alongside community innovation prototyping.
