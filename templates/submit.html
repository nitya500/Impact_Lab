How to Write an INNOTECH-Ready Problem

  • Isolate root causes: Rather than "laptops keep breaking", pinpoint "screen corner hinge fractures caused by 1.2m workbench drops in maker spaces".
  • Set realistic constraints: Clearly stating whether your budget is ₹500 or ₹20,000 prevents solvers from proposing unaffordable overkill.
  • Define measurable success: Numerical targets (e.g. "survive 1.2m drop", "maintain 2-8°C for 48 hrs") allow objective testing.
  • No solution needed: Over half of high-impact innovations begin with problem owners who simply documented a critical friction point.
