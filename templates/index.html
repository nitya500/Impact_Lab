Three Primary Workflows to Tested Impact

Whether you have an idea ready to evaluate, need a fresh solution, or are looking for hands-on implementers, IMPACT LAB has a dedicated path for you.

Workflow A

I Have a Problem & a Solution Idea

Submit both your challenge and initial solution concept. Our transparent Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) calculates a feasibility score across 6 key operational dimensions plus risk factors.

  • Instant algorithmic scoring (0-100)
  • Identifies positive drivers and execution bottlenecks
  • Provides actionable improvement recommendations
Submit & Evaluate Idea →
Workflow B

I Have a Problem but No Solution

Describe your challenge in natural language. Our NLP vector space engine normalizes your text, extracts meaningful terms, and recommends proven engineering and community blueprints.

  • TF-IDF Vectorization and Cosine Similarity ranking
  • Transparent relevance percentages and keyword match overlap
  • Curated fallback patterns when no direct match exists
Discover Solutions →
Workflow C

I Need Help Implementing a Solution

Stuck on execution? Publish a targeted help request for Python scripting, 3D CAD modeling, hardware sensor calibration, or community logistics to connect directly with volunteer solvers.

  • Browse and post requests by specific technical skills
  • Verified solver directory of students and domain mentors
  • Track request lifecycle from Open to In Progress to Resolved

Methodology

The 5-Step Problem-to-Impact Lifecycle

A rigorous, engineering-minded journey replacing guesswork with verified problem definition and field validation.

01

Define Challenge

Structure the problem with target stakeholders, operational constraints, available resources, and quantitative success criteria.

02

NLP Analysis

Tokenize and normalize text, eliminate stopwords, extract TF-IDF keywords, and detect civic or government portal relevance.

03

Match Solutions

Search candidate solutions via cosine similarity, review explainable match rationales, or browse domain engineering blueprints.

04

MCDA Evaluation

Score viability across 6 weighted dimensions (benefit, tech feasibility, cost, speed, resources, scalability) minus risk penalties.

05

Test & Measure

Run a localized pilot, record actual measured results in SQLite, and transparently document verified real-world impact.
